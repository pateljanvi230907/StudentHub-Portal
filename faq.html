Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Enter your registered email address, password, and select your user type, then click the Login button.

Go to the Events page, choose an event, click the Register button, fill out the registration form, and submit it.

Open the Attendance page from the navigation menu to view your attendance percentage for each subject.

Visit the Assignments page, choose the subject, upload your assignment file, and click Upload.

Open the Materials page and click the Download link for the required notes or study resources.

Go to the Results page to view your marks, CGPA, and download your result PDF.

Click the Profile option from the navigation menu and edit your personal information.

Click the "Forgot Password" link on the login page and follow the instructions to reset your password.
